Key Findings

Data-Driven Insights

Critical findings from our product placement analysis

Checkout Placement Delivers Highest ROI

Products placed at checkout counters show an average ROI of 18.4x — significantly outperforming all other placement types due to lower costs and high impulse-buy conversion rates.

18.4x ROI

Eye-Level Shelf Drives Consistent Sales

Shelf eye-level placement consistently delivers the best balance of volume and revenue, with 9.2x ROI and the highest repeat purchase rate across all demographics.

9.2x ROI

Plot Integration Maximizes Brand Recall

In film placements, plot-integrated products achieve 95% brand recall — more than double that of background placements (38%). Combined with merchandise, revenue exceeds $1.25M per campaign.

95% Recall

25-34 Age Group is the Power Segment

Consumers aged 25-34 represent the highest-value segment — they shop more frequently, spend more per visit, and are the most responsive to strategic placement across all channels.

$58 Avg Spend
